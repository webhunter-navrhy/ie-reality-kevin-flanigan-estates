Exceptional 6-Bedroom Detached Family Residence

Discover this impressive detached family home, offering six spacious bedrooms, a dedicated study, and generous living accommodation designed for modern family life. Beautifully presented with a low-maintenance red brick exterior, the property features two bright reception rooms, a bright sunroom overlooking the rear garden, kitchen, utility room and guest wc. The first floor has 4 bedrooms (master bedroom ensuite) and study and a family bathroom. The second floor has two large bedrooms complete with a walk-in wardrobe. Additional features include oil-fired central heating, a bottled gas hob, a detached garage with an electric door, and a cobblelocked driveway.

Situated within a highly sought-after residential estate, this exceptional home enjoys a superb location just 2 km west of Blackrock Village and 4 km south of Dundalk Town Centre. Residents benefit from easy access to a wide range of local amenities, including Blackrock's shops, cafés and restaurants, Felda Health Fitness & Spa, and Dundalk Golf Club. The M1 Motorway is only minutes away, providing a convenient commute to Dublin Airport in approximately 40 minutes, while regular train and bus services from Dundalk offer excellent transport links to Dublin and beyond.
